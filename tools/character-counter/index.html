How to use this Character Counter Tool:

  • Type or paste your text into the input box
  • Click Count Characters to analyze your text (auto-counts as you type)
  • View detailed statistics about your text
  • Copy your text or clear the input as needed

About Character Counting:

  • Total characters: Counts all characters including letters, numbers, symbols, and spaces
  • Characters (no spaces): Counts only visible characters excluding spaces
  • Words: Counts all sequences of characters separated by spaces
  • Lines: Counts the number of lines in your text
  • Bytes: Calculates the size of your text in bytes (UTF-8 encoding)

All processing is done in your browser - your text never leaves your computer
