Understanding Salary Increases:

Salary hikes are typically expressed as a percentage increase over your current salary. Our calculator helps you:

  • Plan your finances - See exactly how much more you'll earn after a raise
  • Negotiate better - Understand what different percentage increases mean in dollar terms
  • Compare job offers - Calculate equivalent salaries when considering new positions
  • Budget effectively - See your new monthly take-home amount

Salary Negotiation Tips:

  • Research industry standards for your role and experience level
  • Consider total compensation (bonuses, benefits, stock options)
  • Time your negotiation appropriately (after strong performance or during review cycles)
  • Practice your talking points and be prepared with data
  • Be professional and positive throughout the process
