How to use this Epoch Converter:

  • Select the type of conversion you want to perform
  • Enter the timestamp or date as required
  • Select the time unit (seconds, milliseconds, etc.)
  • Click Convert to see the results
  • Use the copy buttons to save the results

About Unix Timestamps:

  • Unix Timestamp: Count of seconds since Jan 1, 1970 (UTC)
  • Milliseconds: Timestamp multiplied by 1000
  • Microseconds: Timestamp multiplied by 1,000,000
  • Nanoseconds: Timestamp multiplied by 1,000,000,000
  • All conversions account for your local timezone

All conversions happen in your browser - we never store or transmit your data
