How to use this Text-to-Speech tool:

  • Enter or paste your text in the text area
  • Select a voice from the dropdown menu
  • Adjust speed, pitch, and volume as needed
  • Click Play to hear the speech
  • Use Pause/Stop to control playback
  • Click Download to save as an audio file

Features:

  • Multiple Voices: Choose from different system voices
  • Customizable Speech: Adjust speed, pitch and volume
  • Real-time Playback: Hear changes immediately
  • Cross-browser Support: Works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari

All processing is done in your browser - your text never leaves your computer
