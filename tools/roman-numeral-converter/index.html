About Roman Numerals:

  • Basic Symbols: I (1), V (5), X (10), L (50), C (100), D (500), M (1000)
  • Subtractive Notation: IV (4), IX (9), XL (40), XC (90), CD (400), CM (900)
  • Additive Notation: III (3), XX (20), CC (200), MM (2000)
  • Large Numbers: This converter supports values up to 3,999,999
  • No Zero: The Romans had no symbol for zero
