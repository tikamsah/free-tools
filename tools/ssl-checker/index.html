About SSL Certificates:

  • Valid Certificate: Properly issued, not expired, and trusted by browsers
  • Expiration: Certificates typically last 1-2 years before needing renewal
  • Issuer: Certificate Authority (CA) that issued the certificate
  • Subject: Domain name(s) the certificate is valid for
  • Protocols: Modern sites should support TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3
