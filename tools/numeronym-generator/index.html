What are Numeronyms?

Numeronyms are abbreviations where numbers are used to shorten words by replacing middle letters. They follow the pattern of first letter + number of middle letters + last letter.

Examples:

Internationalization
i18n
Localization
l10n
Accessibility
a11y
Hello world
