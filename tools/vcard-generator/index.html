How to use this vCard Generator:

  • Fill in your contact information (at least first name, last name and email are required)
  • Click Generate vCard to create your digital business card
  • Download the .vcf file or scan the QR code to save the contact
  • Share your vCard with others via email, messaging apps, or print the QR code

About vCards:

  • vCard (VCF) is a standard file format for electronic business cards
  • Supported by all major contact management systems and email clients
  • Can be imported directly into address books on smartphones and computers
  • Version 3.0 is widely supported and includes all common contact fields

All data stays in your browser - we never store or transmit your contact information
