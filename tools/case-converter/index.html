How to use this Case Converter:

  • Enter your text in the input box
  • Select your desired case conversion option
  • Click Convert to transform your text
  • Use the Copy button to copy the results
  • Click any case option to quickly switch between formats

About Case Types:

  • lowercase: Converts all characters to lowercase
  • UPPERCASE: Converts all characters to uppercase
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of the first word in each sentence
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the First Letter of Each Word
  • camelCase: firstWordLowercaseThenCapitalized
  • PascalCase: EachWordCapitalizedNoSpaces
  • snake_case: words_separated_by_underscores
  • kebab-case: words-separated-by-hyphens
  • tOGGLE cASE: iNVERTS tHE cASE oF eACH cHARACTER
  • iNVERSE cASE: First character lowercase, rest uppercase

All conversions happen in your browser - we never store or transmit your data
