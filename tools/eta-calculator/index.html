About the ETA Calculator

This tool helps you calculate the estimated time of arrival based on:

  • Distance - The total distance to your destination
  • Average speed - Your expected average travel speed
  • Departure time - When you plan to start your journey
  • Stops - Number of planned stops and their duration

How to Use

  1. Select your preferred unit system (Metric or Imperial)
  2. Enter the distance to your destination
  3. Enter your expected average speed
  4. Set your departure date and time
  5. Add any planned stops and their duration
  6. Click "Calculate ETA" to see your estimated arrival time

Practical Applications

This calculator is useful for:

  • Road trips - Plan your journey and arrival time
  • Logistics - Estimate delivery times
  • Commuting - Calculate when you'll arrive at work or home
  • Event planning - Coordinate arrival times with others
  • Fitness activities - Estimate running or cycling times
